# Code of conduct

Murmur contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe, focused, and respectful.
This applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and project coordination
channels whenever someone represents the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be specific about the work and generous about the person doing it.
- Give review feedback with evidence, reproducible examples, and a practical path forward.
- Welcome different levels of experience, identities, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Respect privacy, consent, attribution, licensing, and responsible security disclosure.
- Stop behavior when asked, acknowledge impact, and repair harm where possible.
- Keep disagreement on product, architecture, safety, and evidence; do not turn it into harassment.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate intimidation,
personal attacks, or publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Neither is
using project access, automation, or credentials to retaliate against a contributor.

## Enforcement

Report public low-risk moderation concerns to a maintainer without repeating harmful content. For a
private or safety-sensitive report, use the repository's
[private reporting channel](https://github.com/mattpatagon/murmur/security/advisories/new) and label
the report as conduct-related. Do not expose personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed for a fair review, consider context and impact,
and may edit or remove content, warn participants, restrict participation, or permanently ban an
account. Maintainers must recuse themselves from reports in which they are directly involved when
another maintainer is available. Deliberately false reports or retaliation may themselves result in
enforcement.
